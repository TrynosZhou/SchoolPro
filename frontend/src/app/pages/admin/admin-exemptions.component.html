Finance

Fee exemptions

Staff children are exempt from desk fee, registration fee and tuition. A 100% exemption writes off the student's whole outstanding balance. A fixed percentage is calculated on the original tuition invoice amount and deducted from the invoice balance, so the student pays the reduced tuition for the term. A fixed amount is deducted from the outstanding balance. The same discount applies when bulk tuition invoices are created and on registration tuition lines.
